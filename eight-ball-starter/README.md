# Theme Name: Magic 8 Ball
Guch's Magic 8 Ball Experiment

## Project Overview
It's an interactive website where you can tell the Magic 8 Ball about your fortunes.

## Design Decisions
I made it a space style website with neon colors

## My Answers
0. "It is certain" (Positive)
1. "It is decidedly so" (Positive)
2. "Reply hazy try again" (Neutral)
3. "Cannot predict now" (Neutral)
4. "Do not count on it" (Negative)
5. "My sources say no" (Negative)
6. "Outlook not so good" (Negative)
7. "Signs point to yes" (Positive)

## My Answer Types
Positive: To show that the 8 ball gave you a positive response
Neutral: To show that the 8 ball is still thinking about it
Negative: To show that the 8 ball gave you a negative response
